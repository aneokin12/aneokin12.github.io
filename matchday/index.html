Kit room

Eight players, eight kits, one move from the keeper's hands to the back of the net. Each touch is a stop in my career.

or go round to the kit room →

Keys

l / j / → / space
Play the ball to the next player. Press again to skip to the freeze frame.
h / k / ←
Go back to the previous player
1 – 8
Jump straight to a shirt number
gg / G
Back to the keeper, or on to the final whistle
r
Replay the last pass
m
Have the commentary read aloud
t
Switch between daylight and floodlights
s
Turn the crowd noise on or off
ctrl + space
Open the toolbar: every action, player and link in one place. Type to narrow it down.
esc
Close whatever is open, then leave the ground

Set in Anybody, Instrument Sans, and Martian Mono. The stadium is three.js.
