    Keys

    h / l
    Move along the rail or the trophy shelf. Arrow keys work too.
    j / k
    Down to the trophies, up to the kits, and up again to my favourite players
    f
    Flip the selected shirt to see the name and number
    enter
    Open the selected item's link
    gg / G
    First kit, last trophy
    ctrl + space
    Open the toolbar: every kit, trophy, player, action and link in one place. Type to narrow it down.
    t
    Switch to the plain squad list
    p
    Walk over to the record player, or step away from it
    esc
    Close whatever is open, then step back: from a player to the hall of fame, from anywhere else to the whole room

    Set in Anybody, Instrument Sans, and Martian Mono. The room is three.js.
